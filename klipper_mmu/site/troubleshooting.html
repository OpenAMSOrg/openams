When things go wrong  /  faults, findings and recovery

It stopped. Here is why.

When the unit finds a problem it cannot handle safely, it stops every motor, keeps the fault until you acknowledge it, and waits. It does the same if it hears nothing from Klipper for 2 seconds. It does not guess, and it does not retry with a jam in the tube.

Ask first

KLIPPER_MMU_DIAGNOSE MMU=box

It reads every switch, the buffer and the drivers, and prints one finding per problem: what it concerns, what it probably means, and what to do. It never moves anything. With nothing wrong:

klipper_mmu box: no problems found; state=idle path_occupied=False hub=open

The same checks also run after every load, unload and calibration, and print anything new.

The recovery order

After a fault, go through these in order. Skipping ahead gets a refusal.

  1. Diagnose. Read the cause.
  2. Acknowledge. ACK_FAULT.
  3. Fix the cause. If a driver failed, RESTART_DRIVER LANE=n (or SELECTOR=1) until it reports ready.
  4. Say where the filament is. RECONCILE_PATH ACTION=CLEAR if the tube is empty, ACTION=ADOPT LANE=n if lane n is in it.
  5. Start fresh. RECOVER_SESSION.
KLIPPER_MMU_DIAGNOSE MMU=box
KLIPPER_MMU_ACK_FAULT MMU=box
KLIPPER_MMU_RESTART_DRIVER MMU=box LANE=n
KLIPPER_MMU_RECONCILE_PATH MMU=box ACTION=CLEAR
KLIPPER_MMU_RECOVER_SESSION MMU=box

Step 4 also clears OpenAMS's record of the fault and prints OpenAMS lane cleared. An older OpenAMS asks you to run RESTART (not FIRMWARE_RESTART) instead. The recovery commands need path_reconciliation: True and driver_restart: True in [klipper_mmu box].

A load fails

A load gets up to three attempts within 240 s, backing off and changing tactic between them. The log gets one line per attempt: which segment, what went wrong, how far the filament got, and what it tried next.

Report saysMeaningCheck
lane to HUB: no HUB after …Filament left the lane but never reached the hub.Tip catching at the hub entry (cut it at an angle), a stuck or tangled spool, or the drive not gripping.
compressed … before the calibrated minimumArrived too early.A kink, broken filament or unseated fitting. If you shortened the tube, measure it again.
no resistance within … past HUBNever arrived.Tube connections first, then a slipping drive, then the buffer.
One lane needs more distance than the othersThat lane's drive is slipping.Tension, clean gear teeth, a heavy or binding spool. Do not recalibrate the tube to hide it.
Every lane longer or shorterThe tube changed.Measure it again.

Switches

SymptomLikely cause and fix
Reports filament when there is noneReversed polarity, or leftover filament. Flip the switch's active level in the pin menu (like !).
Reads unknown or changes by itselfFloating input. Turn on its pull-up (like ^) and check the connector.
BouncesMany flickers while nothing moves: loose connector, bad crimp or worn lever. Or someone is pushing filament by hand.
Hub occupied, but no lane loadedSomething is stuck in the hub (filament, a string, a lever that did not return). Look inside; do not eject every lane.
LOAD switch made, BAY switch openThe BAY lever, its wiring or polarity, or filament fed in without passing the BAY switch.

After a pin-menu change, build, flash and run the switch check again.

Buffer

0 at rest is normal. The buffer check tests all of these in about 10 seconds.

SymptomLikely cause and fix
High with nothing loadedMoved magnet, or a wrong or reversed calibration. Calibrate again.
Invalid or stale readingBuffer cable or its two signal wires.
Does not return after compressionSticking slide. Clean it.

Motor drivers

MessageLikely cause and fix
“Board-startup driver fault”Motor power was off when the board started (the board runs on USB; the drivers do not), or a loose cable. Power on, then FIRMWARE_RESTART.
Driver reset itselfUsually static from filament in PTFE. Ground the motor screws or frame.
UndervoltageMotor supply off or sagging. Check the 24 V.
Shorted phaseDamaged or pinched motor cable.
OverheatedImprove cooling or lower the motor current.
“Possible open load”A hint only; the chip reports it unreliably at standstill and at speed. Check the cable.

Fix the cause, then KLIPPER_MMU_RESTART_DRIVER MMU=box LANE=n. It never switches a motor on; the stopped operation still needs the rest of the recovery order.

Lost connection

After 2 seconds without Klipper the unit stops. Usual causes: a host too busy (webcam, timelapse, a slicer on the same Pi), or an unreliable USB cable or CAN bus. Fix the cause, then run KLIPPER_MMU_RECOVER_SESSION MMU=box. If DIAGNOSE reports a stranded preload, unpreload or eject, it names RECOVER_LOCAL or RECOVER_WAITER first.

Which lane is in the tube?

More than one lane's LOAD made with HUB made

Two lanes have filament at their LOAD switch and the hub is occupied. Switches cannot tell which one went through, so the unit asks. Look, then run RECONCILE_PATH ACTION=ADOPT LANE=n, or ACTION=CLEAR if you emptied the tube. Neither moves anything.

The opposite case, a lane the unit believes loaded whose switches now read empty, is usually a runout, broken filament or a failed switch.

Everything else

SymptomLikely cause and fix
Follow fault on an encoder unit while printingThe gear moved 10 mm (the default detection length) with no encoder click: a clog, a slipping gear, or an encoder fault. Clear the path and check the encoder wheel.
“gate N is not calibrated”That selector gate has no saved position or angle. See Calibration.
Selector home failedCheck the endstop (or, on indexed selectors, the gate's switch) and that nothing blocks the selector.
Spool does not wind backRespooler turns the wrong way (swap its drive pins), slips, or is dead or disconnected.
The unit “missed its own timing”Not a wiring problem: the board could not keep up. Note what you were doing and report it.
