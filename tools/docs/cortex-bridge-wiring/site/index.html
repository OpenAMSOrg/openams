Wiring the Cortex Bridge

Connect the bridge to your Klipper computer, the printer’s 24 V power supply and the original Bambu Lab AMS filament buffer, and Klipper can drive a stock Bambu Lab AMS.

Connector details

Two connectors take cables you wire yourself. Two more stay empty. The Cortex Bridge board has the full connector list.

P1

PWR IN

Green 2-way screw terminal, 5.08 mm pitch, lower left of the board. Take its 24 V from spare terminals on the printer’s 24 V power supply, with the printer switched off.

PinMarkConnect to
1++24 V terminal on the printer’s supply
2−0 V (−) terminal on the printer’s supply

The + and − are marked on the board. A 5 A fuse sits next to the terminal. The supply needs spare capacity for the AMS units, which draw most while drying.

A separate 24 V DC supply also works; wire it the same way.

CN1

BUFFER

4-pin, 3.0 mm pitch, 2 × 2, Micro-Fit 3.0 style (“the 4-pin Molex”), right edge of the board. It goes to the 4-pin connector on the original Bambu Lab AMS filament buffer.

PinSignal
1+24 V out
20 V / ground
3RS-485 P
4RS-485 N
These are connector pin numbers, not a left-to-right order. Plug and unplug this cable only with the power off.
Leave empty

J4 PRGRM, the small white 6-pin connector on the top edge, and J7, the 5-pin header at the bottom right. Connect nothing to them.

Wiring order

Power off first. Switch the printer and its 24 V power supply off, and unplug the bridge’s USB, before you connect anything.

  1. 24 V to PWR IN P1

    With the printer switched off, run two wires from spare terminals on the printer’s 24 V power supply: + to +, − to −. The supply needs spare capacity for the AMS units, which draw most while drying.

  2. BUFFER CN1 to the buffer

    Connect the 4-pin buffer cable to the 4-pin connector on the original Bambu Lab AMS filament buffer.

    Before you use the AMS, fit the stronger spring supplied with the bridge in the buffer. See Buffer spring.

  3. Buffer to the AMS

    Connect the buffer’s 6-pin connector to the first AMS with the stock Bambu Lab 6-pin cable. The buffer connects to the first AMS only.

    Start with one AMS. Add more after the first one works, up to 4 in a chain: buffer → first AMS → second → third → fourth, each with another 6-pin cable from the previous AMS’s 6-pin port.

    Lay the filament path with 3 mm ID / 4 mm OD PTFE tube from the AMS to the coupler (see PTFE tube). With one AMS, run its tube straight to the buffer. With 2 to 4, run each AMS’s tube into the printed PTFE splitter and one tube from the splitter to the buffer. From the buffer, the tube goes into the “3” side of the PTFE tube coupler v5, just before the printer. From its “2” side, 3 mm ID tube, or 2 mm ID tube if your extruder needs it, runs to the toolhead.

  4. USB-C J1 to the Klipper computer

    Use a USB data cable (USB-A to USB-C) from a USB-A port on the Klipper computer itself, not a hub with its own power switch. A charge-only cable does not work.

  5. Leave PRGRM J4 and J7 empty

    Nothing connects to them.

On

Then switch the printer and its 24 V supply on, and check that all three LEDs are on.

PTFE tube: 3 mm ID / 4 mm OD only

Use tube that is 3 mm inside and 4 mm outside for AMS to splitter, splitter to buffer, and buffer to coupler. Narrower tube (such as the common 2 mm ID) adds too much friction for the AMS to push filament to the toolhead reliably.

Everything from the AMS to the coupler must be 3 mm ID. After the coupler, run 3 mm ID tube to the toolhead, or 2 mm ID tube if your extruder’s entrance needs it (for example the Orbiter). Plug the 3 mm tube into the coupler’s “3” side and the toolhead tube into its “2” side. The coupler’s taper keeps the filament from catching on the 1 mm step between the two sizes.

Power-on check

With everything connected, all three LEDs are on.

ColourLEDOn meansIf it stays off
White D9 USB 5 V is present. Check the USB cable and the USB port.
Green D10 The bridge’s 3.3 V is running. It needs white on first. If white is on and green stays off, contact support.
Red D8 24 V is on the BUFFER output. Check the printer’s 24 V supply, the polarity at PWR IN, and the 5 A fuse.

The case and what you need

The bridge fits a 3D-printed case that sticks to the printer’s rear panel. Print and assemble it, then wire it as above.

Buffer spring: fit it before using the AMS

Every Cortex Bridge comes with a stronger replacement spring for the original Bambu Lab filament buffer. Open the buffer the way Bambu Lab’s buffer slider and spring steps show, swap the original, weaker spring for the supplied one, and close it. Then check that the slider moves freely and springs back.

Why: the stronger spring lets the buffer push back against the longer PTFE tube runs of a Klipper printer without triggering early.

The assembled case. The grey part underneath is the optional DIN clip.

Assembly

  1. 1Lid
  2. 2Cortex Bridge board
  3. 3Base
  4. 4Three M2 × 10 case screws, from below
  5. 5Two M2 × 6 countersunk screws for the DIN clip, from inside the base
  6. 6DIN clip Optional

  1. Optional DIN clip

    Screw it to the outside of the base first, with two M2 × 6 countersunk screws driven from inside the base.

  2. Board into the base

    Lay the board in the base, its three mounting holes on the base’s standoffs.

  3. Lid on

    Its notches go over the USB-C, BUFFER and PRGRM connectors.

  4. Screws from below

    Turn it over and drive the three M2 × 10 screws through the base and the board into the lid. Snug, not tight: the lid is plastic.

  5. LED light guides

    Push a piece of 1.75 mm clear filament into each of the three LED holes in the lid until it stops on the LED, then cut it flush with the roof. The roof-to-LED depth is about 15.7 mm.

  6. Mount it

    Stick the foam tape to the base, not to the DIN clip, and mount the case on the printer’s rear panel.

Mounting

The default is double-sided foam tape on the bottom of the base, stuck to the rear panel of the printer, within reach of the printer’s 24 V supply and the buffer cable. Clean the panel first and press for a few seconds. The DIN clip is an optional alternative for printers with a DIN rail, for example under the printer. If Bambu Lab’s short 4-pin cable from the buffer doesn’t reach that far, add a BIQU 4-pin 1 m extension (BIQU, Amazon) between it and the bridge: one 4-pin run from the buffer to the bridge.
