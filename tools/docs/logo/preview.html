OpenAMS logo

Four filaments, one for each AMS bay, spiral inside a circle, each led by its tip. Line and dot share a colour; the four colours are the docs' spool colours. Every direction is drawn from one generator. A3, "Rim" is the chosen mark: each spool lets its filament go at the rim.

Shown in the page's light or dark theme. Parameters are in viewBox units: the mark is 100 wide, so 1 unit is 0.16 px at 16 px.
